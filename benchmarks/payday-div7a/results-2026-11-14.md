The 31 questions in [questions.json](questions.json) were scored with the unchanged offline scorer (`bench score --json`). The Codex round used 3 fresh repeats per condition. The validated Opus 5.5 replication used 10 fresh repeats per condition, with registered extra repetitions reported separately. Tools and web access were off. Figures are means per 31-question call. Different runners and models are reported separately and are not pooled.

Its guide comparison and claimed guide effect are withdrawn. The validated Messages API replication below replaces that comparison. Each phase's method was registered before its model calls.

## Validated Opus 5.5 replication

`claude-opus-5-5` at high effort through the first-party Messages API: 20 primary calls, 10 per condition. The full-guide system contained the same neutral system as the bare arm plus the complete 97,441-character guide exactly once. Both arms received identical questions and the full answer schema, with a 16,000-token output limit. Actual submitted system/user hashes, model, effort, output limit and absence of tools were checked before dispatch and again during scoring.

After a separately registered native-schema attempt encountered the provider's grammar-size limit, both arms used the original question prompt with the unchanged JSON schema appended. Strict local parsing rejected duplicate keys, missing/extra fields or IDs and incorrect types without repair or retry. The stopped technical attempts are excluded from the comparison.

## Reading these results

- The guide tells a model to check current sources before relying on a figure. This closed-book setup cannot satisfy that, so a guide arm's abstention does not show that the bare model is more correct in practice.
- The corrected Opus primary guide calls and the Codex guide calls all abstained. Extra Opus guide trials sometimes answered. Different runners, models and response formats prevent a general comparison across rounds.
- These are 31 public cases on 2 topics, written by the publisher. Repeating them does not establish a general model ranking or accounting accuracy.
- The keys share a calendar and engines with the publisher's products, as the [benchmark README](README.md) discloses. The corrected guide comparison is publisher-run development evidence, not an independent holdout.
- No tax advice is given and the underlying law was not verified again for these runs.

The model answers, prompts, usage records and correction audit are kept by the publisher and are not in this repository.
