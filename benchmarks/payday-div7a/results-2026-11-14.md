# Model results, 14 November 2026

Two rounds scored the 31 questions in [questions.json](questions.json) with the unchanged offline scorer (`bench score --json`) on 14 November 2026. Each compared the questions alone with the questions plus the verbatim [drdebits.md](https://github.com/ryanduguid/DrDebits/blob/08eb0fa2ebcba55a049a19875cf0c54ee5e21982/drdebits.md) guide at the commit below, with tools and web access off and 3 fresh repeats per condition. The method was committed before any model call. The Opus round was added after the Codex round, with the method unchanged. Figures are means per 31-question call. The rounds used different runners and models, so they are reported separately and not pooled.

Inputs: DrDebits commit `08eb0fa2ebcba55a049a19875cf0c54ee5e21982`. The same questions, guide, key, answer schema and scorer were used in both rounds. Before the first round, `bench keys` recomputed 21 keys and matched all 31 recorded engine outputs; 10 keys are source-derived. The inputs were unchanged for the second.

## Reading these results

- The guide tells a model to check current sources before relying on a figure. This closed-book setup cannot satisfy that, so a guide arm's abstention does not show that the bare model is more correct in practice.
- The Opus guide arms did not abstain as the Codex guide arms did. The runners and models differ, so that contrast cannot be put down to the guide alone.
- 31 public cases on 2 topics, with 3 repeats, establish no model ranking and no general accounting accuracy.
- The publisher wrote the questions and keys, and the keys share a calendar and engines with the publisher's products, as the [benchmark README](README.md) discloses.
- No tax advice is given and the underlying law was not verified again for these runs.

The model answers, prompts and usage records are kept by the publisher and are not in this repository.
