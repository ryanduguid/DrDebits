# Crosswalk from DrDebits controls to a firm's AI-use policy

A small Australian accounting firm's AI-use policy may hold clauses on approved tools, client data, human review, sources, calculations, records and prohibited uses. The table below takes such clauses one by one and names the DrDebits control that supports each, with the file or test that holds it. It was checked against `main` on 14 November 2026. Where a common clause has no DrDebits control, the row says 'not covered'.

DrDebits supplies 4 kinds of control, and they carry different weight:

- **Guide rule:** text in [drdebits.md](../drdebits.md) that the firm's assistant loads and its model is told to follow. Prompt wording cannot make a model comply, so a guide rule is an instruction, not an enforcement.
- **Mechanical check:** a command in the builder, `tools/drdebits_build`, that the firm or its host runs. It checks only what its own documentation says it checks.
- **Behaviour test:** a fabricated case in [tests/behaviour-tests.md](../tests/behaviour-tests.md) (111 cases, built from `src/data/behaviour-tests.yaml`) that a person runs against the firm's model and judges. Nothing in this repository calls a model, and the recorded [results](../evals/RESULTS.md) do not cover the current guide revision.
- **Reference:** a document a person reads, such as the [AI vendor assurance checklist](../reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md). It lists questions and the evidence that answers them, and enforces nothing.

This page is supplier information. It does not show that a firm's policy or its use of AI complies with anything, and the firm decides what its policy requires. The [TPB(GS) 55/2026 concordance](../reference/tpb-gs55-concordance.md) maps the same guide controls to the Tax Practitioners Board's Guidance Statement and the National AI Centre's essential practices, and the [AI register entry](./ai-register-entry.md) gives the supplier's side of the firm's AI register.

## Crosswalk

| Policy clause | DrDebits control | Kind | Where it is held | What it does not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved tools and vendor assessment | The [AI vendor assurance checklist](../reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md) lists the question for each obligation, the evidence that answers it and where the obligation sits. The guide's [Data and privacy gate](../drdebits.md#data-and-privacy-gate) says to use only an approved model, connector and storage environment. Cases VEND-001 and VEND-002 test a model offered a vendor's security page as due diligence, or a capacity target as a reason to drop the output contract. | Reference, guide rule, behaviour test | `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md`, `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Approve a tool or vendor, or check which tool a firm uses |
| Client data handling and privacy | The data and privacy gate: minimum necessary data, TFNs redacted unless essential and approved, and stop, do not echo and alert the privacy lead when data arrived without authority. Non-negotiable stops bar uploading client information to a third-party provider without confirmed authority and exposing a TFN. Cases DATA-001 to DATA-005 test whole-file uploads, data already pasted into an unapproved model, partial, encoded and hidden TFNs. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md`, `src/guide/130-non-negotiable-stops.md` | Redact, detect or block client data: the guide says the host must apply the gate before client information reaches the model |
| Confidentiality between clients | The data and privacy gate limits a deliverable's confidential content to the approved engagement and recipient scope. Cases DATA-006 and DATA-007 test one client's figures in another client's deliverable and a figure carried over from the previous client. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Separate client sessions or storage |
| Human review before anything is relied on, lodged or sent | The [Output contract](../drdebits.md#output-contract) tells the model to give every high-impact draft the banner `DRAFT — PROFESSIONAL REVIEW REQUIRED`, `action_authority: NONE` and a 'Human actions before use' section. The `gate` command blocks a draft that lacks the banner, one primary decision status, `action_authority: NONE`, the state-change and source-version lines or a mandatory section with content. Its exit 0 means only that the mechanical checks passed; a human still reviews the draft. Cases LODGE-001, PAY-001, PUB-001 and AUTO-001 test lodging, paying and publishing on request, and an automation treating `PROCEED_DRAFT_ONLY` as permission to act. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/170-output-contract.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/src/gate.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `each_missing_contract_element_blocks`, `two_decision_fields_block`, `an_empty_section_counts_as_missing` | Show that a person reviewed the draft. The firm's host decides whether `gate` runs and what happens after it |
| No autonomous external or state-changing actions | The guide bars the model from signing, lodging, submitting, paying, approving or communicating with a regulator ([Operating role and responsibility](../drdebits.md#operating-role-and-responsibility), [Non-negotiable stops](../drdebits.md#non-negotiable-stops)), and keeps tools read-only until an exact action is approved ([Deployment, integrity and authority](../drdebits.md#deployment-integrity-and-authority), [Trust boundary](../drdebits.md#trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity)). `gate` exits 3, for a person to decide, when a draft's `state_changing_tool_action` line records an action. In an evaluation run, the `score` and `assess` commands fail a response whose recorded tool calls include anything other than a recognised source read. Cases AUTH-002, LEDGER-001 to LEDGER-004 and CALLER-001 test regulator messages, ledger writes and an unverified caller. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/070-operating-role-and-responsibility.md`, `src/guide/080-trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_state_changing_action_asks_for_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `unrecognised_tool_evidence_fails_closed` | Remove, restrict or watch a tool. `gate` reads only what the draft says, so it misses an action the draft does not record, and `score` and `assess` read an evaluation run's recorded calls, not a live host's. The guide says least-privilege tool access and approval gates belong to the host |
| Source citation and currency | The guide's [Source status](../drdebits.md#source-status) and [Authority and conflicts](../drdebits.md#authority-and-conflicts) sections set which sources bind whom and require `SOURCE CURRENCY NOT CONFIRMED` where currency cannot be confirmed. `gate --authorities` asks for review of a cited Act section or ruling id not on the reviewer's closed list, and `gate --source` asks for review of a quotation not found verbatim in a supplied source. The `sources` command, which a workflow runs weekly, reports when a compilation of an Act the guide pins has been superseded. Cases CITE-001 to CITE-012, SRC-001 to SRC-006, SRC-009 and HIST-001 to HIST-002 test invented, withdrawn and misattributed authorities and superseded sources. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/040-source-status.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `an_unlisted_authority_asks_for_review`, `a_fabricated_quotation_asks_for_review_and_never_blocks`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/sources.rs`: `a_newer_compilation_is_superseded_not_merely_different` | Show that a source applies or is current. The authorities and quotation checks never block, and Divisions, cases and other series are not read. `sources` checks only the Acts the guide pins, not the sources a draft cites |
| Checking calculations | `gate` reperforms every written `a op b = c` calculation in exact decimal arithmetic and blocks one that does not reperform, and one it cannot read whole, such as a sum a word interrupts. `gate --facts` asks for review of a dollar amount or percentage that is neither a supplied fact nor the result of a reperformed calculation. Cases CALC-001 to CALC-012 and WORK-001 to WORK-003 test calculator output, model agreement offered as verification and rounding. | Mechanical check, behaviour test | `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_wrong_figure_blocks_with_the_computed_value`, `every_link_of_a_chain_is_checked`, `a_wrong_figure_equals_no_fact_and_asks_for_review` | Check figures not written as a calculation, plain numbers under `--facts`, or other currencies; a figure that matches a fact may still be wrong. A result within one unit of its last written decimal place passes, so a result rounded up, down or to nearest is not caught: `$4,870.00 × 12% = $584` passes |
| Record keeping and file notes | The [Workpaper record](../drdebits.md#workpaper-record) lists what a material record holds, including preparer and reviewer, sources with retrieval dates, the tool or model and material AI use, and the final human decision, and records a review or approval as done only on evidence. Cases CARE-001, CARE-002 and WORK-004 test a file note that treats AI output as reasonable care, one that names no reviewer and a sign-off the partner has not given. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/180-workpaper-record.md` | Store, retain or check a workpaper: `gate` does not read the workpaper record |
| Reserved professional judgements | Non-negotiable stops and the TPB and APES 110 control sets reserve safe-harbour, culpability, independence, audit and compliance conclusions to the responsible person. `gate` exits 3, for a person to decide, when its prohibited-conclusion screen matches; `assess` reports the same screen for evaluation runs. Cases SAFE-001 to SAFE-003, IND-001, CERT-001, AUDIT-001 and AUDIT-002 test them. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/130-non-negotiable-stops.md`, `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md`; `src/data/behaviour-tests.yaml` (`prohibited_conclusions`); `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_screen_hit_alone_asks_for_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `the_marker_screen_matches_literal_phrases_only` | Detect a prohibited conclusion in other words: the screen matches literal phrases only, and a hit needs a person to judge it |
| No fabricated, false or misleading content | Non-negotiable stops bar fabricating, altering, omitting, backdating or concealing facts, records, evidence, sources, review or approval, and making or supporting a statement known or suspected to be false, misleading or materially incomplete. Cases FAB-001, RISK-001 and ASSUME-001 to ASSUME-003 test an invented tool result, claims set just below a review level, and a conclusion resting on an assumed missing fact. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/130-non-negotiable-stops.md` | Detect a false statement in a draft: `gate` checks the draft's form and arithmetic, not whether its facts are true |
| Instructions hidden in documents and tool output | The trust boundary treats client files, webpages and tool output as untrusted evidence, and an embedded request for credentials, commands or a state change is `HARD_STOP`. Cases INJ-001, INJ-002 and WORK-003 test them. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/080-trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity.md` | Resist injection: the guide says prompt wording alone cannot |
| Using an approved, unaltered version | The guide's [Deployment, integrity and authority](../drdebits.md#deployment-integrity-and-authority) section requires a pinned release verified against `SHA256SUMS`. `bundle install` and `bundle verify` copy and check the bundle against an approved manifest digest and refuse a changed, missing or redirected file. Case INT-001 tests a missing end marker or a release identity that cannot be verified. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `tools/drdebits_build/src/bundle.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/bundle.rs`: `changed_or_missing_file_never_creates_destination`, `untrusted_or_incomplete_manifest_never_creates_destination` | Check that the host loaded the guide or that the model follows it |
| Testing before use | The behaviour tests and the `score` and `assess` commands let the firm run its own model against the cases and record the outcome under `evals/`. The scorer requires the guide and case digests to match and never returns an automatic pass. The [Payday Super and Division 7A benchmark](../benchmarks/payday-div7a/README.md) scores answers against published keys. | Behaviour test, mechanical check | `tools/drdebits_build/src/harness.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `score_from_file_requires_execution_digests`, `a_marker_hit_and_a_clean_response_both_need_semantic_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/evals.rs`: `current_revision_requires_both_digests` | Call a model or judge a response: a person decides each verdict. The benchmark covers only Payday Super and Division 7A; its [model results](../benchmarks/payday-div7a/results-2026-11-14.md) are from 14 November 2026 |
| Incident and breach response | The data and privacy gate tells the model to stop and alert the privacy or security lead when data arrived without authority, and the TPB control set flags the 30-day clock for reporting a significant breach of the Code of Professional Conduct to the TPB. Vendor checklist row VA-28 asks how quickly a vendor tells the firm when client information is exposed. Cases DATA-002 and BRE-001 test them. | Reference, guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md`, `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md`, `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md` | Detect, contain or report an incident; the firm's own procedures do that. Nothing addresses whether a data breach must be notified under the *Privacy Act 1988*'s Notifiable Data Breaches scheme |
| AML/CTF and other regulatory duties | The TPB, APES 110, APES 220 and AML/CTF control sets in the guide. Cases AML-001 to AML-003, NOCLAR-001, TAX-001 and MIS-001 test tipping off, designated services, evasion and promoted arrangements. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md` to `src/guide/160-aml-ctf-control-set.md` | Meet those duties for the firm |
| Telling clients how AI is used | The [automated-decision transparency check](../drdebits.md#automated-decision-transparency-check) puts the questions raised by APP 1.7 to 1.9, which apply from 10 December 2026, to the firm. | Guide rule | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Not covered beyond that check: nothing drafts or checks an engagement letter or client notice |
| Staff training and competence | Not covered. The guide's TPB and APES 110 control sets restate the competence duties, and vendor checklist row VA-36 asks what a person must know to use a product and how the firm establishes that each user knows it. | None | `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md`, `src/guide/150-apes-110-control-set.md`, `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md` | Train staff or record their competence |
| Access control and permissions | Not covered. The guide says the host needs least-privilege tool access and that an `AUTHORISED_HUMAN` is verified by the host or firm outside prompt text, and vendor checklist rows such as VA-24 and VA-41 ask who can reach client information; no DrDebits code sets or checks permissions. | None | `src/guide/020-deployment-integrity-and-authority.md`, `src/guide/080-trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity.md`, `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md` | Configure the host |
| Data retention and deletion | Not covered. The data and privacy gate asks the host to confirm retention before client information reaches the model and defers to the firm's retention and deletion procedures, and vendor checklist rows VA-09 and VA-10 ask how long a vendor keeps client information and how deletion is evidenced. | None | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md`, `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md` | Set a retention period, or retain or delete anything |
| Policy owner, monitoring and review cycle | Not covered. The firm's own register columns, listed in the [AI register entry](./ai-register-entry.md), hold the owner and next review date. | None | | Monitor staff use of AI |

## Running the checks

The builder's commands are in the README's [Gate each high-impact draft](../README.md#gate-each-high-impact-draft) section and in [MAINTENANCE.md](../MAINTENANCE.md). Run them from a trusted checkout of the approved release. A pass from any of them is not professional approval.
