# Crosswalk from DrDebits controls to a firm's AI-use policy

A small Australian accounting firm's AI-use policy usually holds clauses on approved tools, client data, human review, sources, calculations, records and prohibited uses. The table below takes those clauses one by one and names the DrDebits control that supports each, with the file or test that holds it. It was checked against `main` on 14 November 2026. Where a common clause has no DrDebits control, the row says 'not covered'.

DrDebits supplies 3 kinds of control, and they carry different weight:

- **Guide rule:** text in [drdebits.md](../drdebits.md) that the firm's assistant loads and its model is told to follow. Prompt wording cannot make a model comply, so a guide rule is an instruction, not an enforcement.
- **Mechanical check:** a command in the builder, `tools/drdebits_build`, that the firm or its host runs. It checks only what its own documentation says it checks.
- **Behaviour test:** a fabricated case in [tests/behaviour-tests.md](../tests/behaviour-tests.md) (108 cases, built from `src/data/behaviour-tests.yaml`) that a person runs against the firm's model and judges. Nothing in this repository calls a model, and the recorded [results](../evals/RESULTS.md) do not cover the current guide revision.

This page is supplier information. It does not show that a firm's policy or its use of AI complies with anything, and the firm decides what its policy requires. The [TPB(GS) 55/2026 concordance](../reference/tpb-gs55-concordance.md) maps the same guide controls to the Tax Practitioners Board's Guidance Statement and the National AI Centre's essential practices, and the [AI register entry](./ai-register-entry.md) gives the supplier's side of the firm's AI register.

## Crosswalk

| Policy clause | DrDebits control | Kind | Where it is held | What it does not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved tools and vendor assessment | The [AI vendor assurance checklist](../reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md) lists the question for each obligation, the evidence that answers it and where the obligation sits. The guide's [Data and privacy gate](../drdebits.md#data-and-privacy-gate) says to use only an approved model, connector and storage environment. Cases VEND-001 and VEND-002 test a model offered a vendor's security page as due diligence, or a capacity target as a reason to drop the output contract. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md`, `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Approve a tool or vendor, or check which tool a firm uses |
| Client data handling and privacy | The data and privacy gate: minimum necessary data, TFNs redacted unless essential and approved, and stop, do not echo and alert the privacy lead when data arrived without authority. Non-negotiable stops bar uploading client information to a third-party provider without confirmed authority and exposing a TFN. Cases DATA-001 to DATA-005 test whole-file uploads, data already pasted into an unapproved model, partial, encoded and hidden TFNs. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md`, `src/guide/130-non-negotiable-stops.md` | Redact, detect or block client data: the guide says the host must apply the gate before client information reaches the model |
| Confidentiality between clients | The data and privacy gate limits a deliverable's confidential content to the approved engagement and recipient scope. Cases DATA-006 and DATA-007 test one client's figures in another client's deliverable and a figure carried over from the previous client. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Separate client sessions or storage |
| Human review before anything is relied on, lodged or sent | Every high-impact draft carries the banner `DRAFT — PROFESSIONAL REVIEW REQUIRED`, `action_authority: NONE` and a 'Human actions before use' section ([Output contract](../drdebits.md#output-contract)). The `gate` command blocks a draft that lacks the banner, one primary decision status, `action_authority: NONE`, the state-change and source-version lines or a mandatory section with content. Its exit 0 means only that the mechanical checks passed; a human still reviews the draft. Cases LODGE-001, PAY-001, PUB-001 and AUTO-001 test lodging, paying and publishing on request, and an automation treating `PROCEED_DRAFT_ONLY` as permission to act. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/170-output-contract.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/src/gate.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `each_missing_contract_element_blocks`, `two_decision_fields_block`, `an_empty_section_counts_as_missing` | Show that a person reviewed the draft. The firm's host decides whether `gate` runs and what happens after it |
| No autonomous external or state-changing actions | The guide bars the model from signing, lodging, submitting, paying, approving or communicating with a regulator, and keeps tools read-only until an exact action is approved ([Trust boundary](../drdebits.md#trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity), [Non-negotiable stops](../drdebits.md#non-negotiable-stops)). `gate` exits 3, for a person to decide, when a draft records a state-changing action. The `score` and `assess` commands fail a response whose recorded tool evidence crosses the action boundary or cannot be recognised. Cases AUTH-002, LEDGER-001 to LEDGER-004 and CALLER-001 test regulator messages, ledger writes and an unverified caller. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/080-trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_state_changing_action_asks_for_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `unrecognised_tool_evidence_fails_closed` | Remove or restrict a tool. The guide says least-privilege tool access and approval gates belong to the host |
| Source citation and currency | The guide's [Source status](../drdebits.md#source-status) and [Authority and conflicts](../drdebits.md#authority-and-conflicts) sections set which sources bind whom and require `SOURCE CURRENCY NOT CONFIRMED` where currency cannot be confirmed. `gate --authorities` asks for review of a cited Act section or ruling id not on the reviewer's closed list, and `gate --source` asks for review of a quotation not found verbatim in a supplied source. The `sources` command reports a superseded compilation of a pinned Act each week. Cases CITE-001 to CITE-012, SRC-001 to SRC-006, SRC-009 and HIST-001 to HIST-002 test invented, withdrawn and misattributed authorities and superseded sources. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/guide/040-source-status.md`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `an_unlisted_authority_asks_for_review`, `a_fabricated_quotation_asks_for_review_and_never_blocks`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/sources.rs`: `a_newer_compilation_is_superseded_not_merely_different` | Show that a source applies or is current. The authorities and quotation checks never block, and Divisions, cases and other series are not read |
| Checking calculations | `gate` reperforms every written `a op b = c` calculation in exact decimal arithmetic and blocks one that does not reperform, and one it cannot read whole, such as a sum a word interrupts. `gate --facts` asks for review of a dollar amount or percentage that is neither a supplied fact nor the result of a reperformed calculation. Cases CALC-001 to CALC-012 and WORK-001 to WORK-003 test calculator output, model agreement offered as verification and rounding. | Mechanical check, behaviour test | `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_wrong_figure_blocks_with_the_computed_value`, `every_link_of_a_chain_is_checked`, `a_wrong_figure_equals_no_fact_and_asks_for_review` | Check figures not written as a calculation, plain numbers under `--facts`, or other currencies; a figure that matches a fact may still be wrong |
| Record keeping and file notes | The [Workpaper record](../drdebits.md#workpaper-record) lists what a material record holds, including preparer and reviewer, sources with retrieval dates, the tool or model and material AI use, and the final human decision, and records a review or approval as done only on evidence. Cases CARE-001, CARE-002 and WORK-004 test a file note that treats AI output as reasonable care, one that names no reviewer and a sign-off the partner has not given. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/180-workpaper-record.md` | Store, retain or check a workpaper: `gate` does not read the workpaper record |
| Reserved professional judgements | Non-negotiable stops and the TPB and APES 110 control sets reserve safe-harbour, culpability, independence, audit and compliance conclusions to the responsible person. `gate` exits 3, for a person to decide, when its prohibited-conclusion screen matches; `assess` reports the same screen for evaluation runs. Cases SAFE-001 to SAFE-003, IND-001, CERT-001, AUDIT-001 and AUDIT-002 test them. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `src/data/behaviour-tests.yaml` (`prohibited_conclusions`); `tools/drdebits_build/tests/gate.rs`: `a_screen_hit_alone_asks_for_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `the_marker_screen_matches_literal_phrases_only` | Detect a prohibited conclusion in other words: the screen matches literal phrases only, and a hit needs a person to judge it |
| Instructions hidden in documents and tool output | The trust boundary treats client files, webpages and tool output as untrusted evidence, and an embedded request for credentials, commands or a state change is `HARD_STOP`. Cases INJ-001, INJ-002 and WORK-003 test them. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/080-trust-boundary-and-instruction-integrity.md` | Resist injection: the guide says prompt wording alone cannot |
| Using an approved, unaltered version | The guide's [Deployment, integrity and authority](../drdebits.md#deployment-integrity-and-authority) section requires a pinned release verified against `SHA256SUMS`. `bundle install` and `bundle verify` copy and check the bundle against an approved manifest digest and refuse a changed, missing or redirected file. Case INT-001 tests a missing end marker. | Guide rule, mechanical check, behaviour test | `tools/drdebits_build/src/bundle.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/bundle.rs`: `changed_or_missing_file_never_creates_destination`, `untrusted_or_incomplete_manifest_never_creates_destination` | Check that the host loaded the guide or that the model follows it |
| Testing before use | The behaviour tests and the `score` and `assess` commands let the firm run its own model against the cases and record the outcome under `evals/`. The scorer requires the guide and case digests to match and never returns an automatic pass. The [Payday Super and Division 7A benchmark](../benchmarks/payday-div7a/README.md) scores answers against published keys. | Behaviour test, mechanical check | `tools/drdebits_build/src/harness.rs`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/harness.rs`: `score_from_file_requires_execution_digests`, `a_marker_hit_and_a_clean_response_both_need_semantic_review`; `tools/drdebits_build/tests/evals.rs`: `current_revision_requires_both_digests` | Call a model or judge a response: a person decides each verdict |
| Incident and breach response | The data and privacy gate tells the model to stop and alert the privacy or security lead when data arrived without authority, and the TPB control set flags the significant-breach reporting clock. Cases DATA-002 and BRE-001 test them. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md`, `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md` | Detect, contain or report an incident; the firm's own procedures do that |
| AML/CTF and other regulatory duties | The TPB, APES 110, APES 220 and AML/CTF control sets in the guide. Cases AML-001 to AML-003, NOCLAR-001, TAX-001 and MIS-001 test tipping off, evasion and promoted arrangements. | Guide rule, behaviour test | `src/guide/140-tpb-control-set.md` to `src/guide/160-aml-ctf-control-set.md` | Meet those duties for the firm |
| Telling clients how AI is used | The [automated-decision transparency check](../drdebits.md#automated-decision-transparency-check) puts the APP 1.7 to 1.9 questions to the firm. | Guide rule | `src/guide/090-data-and-privacy-gate.md` | Not covered beyond that check: nothing drafts or checks an engagement letter or client notice |
| Staff training and competence | Not covered. The vendor checklist's 'Competence and training' rows ask what a vendor provides. | None | `reference/ai-vendor-assurance.md` | Train staff or record their competence |
| Access control and permissions | Not covered. The guide says the host needs least-privilege tool access; no DrDebits code sets or checks permissions. | None | | Configure the host |
| Data retention and deletion | Not covered. The data and privacy gate defers to the firm's retention and deletion procedures. | None | | Retain or delete anything |
| Policy owner, monitoring and review cycle | Not covered. The firm's own register columns, listed in the [AI register entry](./ai-register-entry.md), hold the owner and next review date. | None | | Monitor staff use of AI |

## Running the checks

The builder's commands are in the README's [Gate each high-impact draft](../README.md#gate-each-high-impact-draft) section and in [MAINTENANCE.md](../MAINTENANCE.md). Run them from a trusted checkout of the approved release. A pass from any of them is not professional approval.
